## About this website

This website is for general information only. It is not an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, an interest in any fund managed or advised by Northwood Partners LLP, and it should not be relied on in making an investment decision. Any offer would be made only through the confidential offering documents of the fund concerned, to investors who qualify under the applicable rules.

## Regulatory status

Northwood Partners LLP is a fictional firm used to demonstrate this website template. A real firm would state its authorisation and registration here, such as its regulator, firm reference number and registered office.

## Fund figures

Performance figures are shown net of fees, expenses and carried interest, as of the date given beside them, and are unaudited unless stated otherwise. Multiples and returns for recent funds are early and may change materially. Past performance is not a guide to future results, and the value of investments can fall as well as rise.

## Portfolio companies

The companies shown are those we are able to talk about publicly. They are not a complete list of the investments in any fund, and they were not chosen on the basis of performance. Their inclusion is not a recommendation. Logos and names belong to the companies concerned.

## Forward-looking statements

Some material on this website, including our letters and essays, contains views about the future. They reflect our opinion when written, may turn out to be wrong, and we do not undertake to update them.
