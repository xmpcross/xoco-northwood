# Changelog

All notable changes to Northwood are documented here.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-21

- Updated Astro to 7.4.1 and other dependencies.
- Updated `AGENTS.md` for Astro 7.
- Files check out with LF line endings on every platform.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-16

- Initial commercial release.
- Astro 7 theme for venture capital and investment firms with home, portfolio, company, team, profile, insights, category archive, entry, search, funds, pitch, about, contact, RSS, robots, sitemap, and 404 routes.
- Companies, team, and insights collections that reference each other, validated at build time.
- Portfolio directory filtered by sector, stage, and status, with search, grid and list views, and filters in the URL.
- Company pages with logo, status, facts, founders, lead partner, funding history, related insights, and companies in the same sector.
- Sector index and a page per sector with thesis, themes, figures, companies, and the team who cover it.
- Team page grouped by role, and profiles listing the companies each person leads and what they have written.
- Insights journal with essays, investor letters, playbooks, and news, and a letter layout with a sign-off from the authors.
- Funds page with firm totals, a fund table, a disclosure, portfolio breakdowns, and realised exits.
- Pitch form with criteria, next steps, and a FAQ.
- Privacy, terms of use, and important information pages.
- About page with a timeline built from milestones, fund closes, and exits.
- Centred reading column, reading progress, share sheet, and search palette on insights.
- Canonical URLs, sitemap, RSS, Open Graph, Twitter/X cards, and JSON-LD.
- Full-width photographic homepage hero with film grain and a centred headline.
- Grainy dark panels with a warm-to-cool glow, and buttons with the arrow in its own box.
- Self-hosted Outfit, monochrome portraits, and a neutral palette with colour kept for accents.
